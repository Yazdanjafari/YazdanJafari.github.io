was it wrong ? which made on a bridge on the sea unbelievably

ChatGPT said:

Yes. "which made on a bridge on the sea unbelievably" is grammatically incorrect.

Here are the problems:

1. ❌ "which made on..."

You need the passive voice.

  • ❌ which made on...

  • ✅ which is built on...

  • ✅ which was built on...

  • ✅ which is located on...

2. ❌ "bridge"

A bridge connects two places.

The Santa Monica amusement park is not on a bridge. It's on a pier.

  • pier = اسکله

  • bridge = پل

3. ❌ "on the sea"

It's more natural to say:

  • ✅ over the Pacific Ocean

  • ✅ on a pier extending over the Pacific Ocean

  • ✅ by the sea (if you're talking about the location in general)

4. ❌ "unbelievably"

This adverb doesn't fit at the end of the sentence.

Instead, say:

  • ✅ which is absolutely incredible.

  • ✅ which is truly amazing.

  • ✅ which I find fascinating.

Natural alternatives

...the famous amusement park, which is built on a pier over the Pacific Ocean.

...the famous amusement park, which was built on a pier extending into the Pacific Ocean.

...the famous amusement park, which is located on a historic pier overlooking the Pacific Ocean.

These are all much more natural and would sound appropriate in an IELTS essay.
